Use Zana everywhere.

On your computer, click the Remote access icon in the bottom-left corner, then Get a connect code.

Sign in with GitHub and enter my-domain. Reserve your address, then copy the connect code.

Back in Zana, paste the code. Your computer connects automatically, and your dedicated address is ready.

Now pick up your iPhone. Open Safari and enter your new Zana address.

Sign in with the same GitHub account, then tap Open Zana.

And voilà! Your agents, right on your phone. Follow their progress from the kanban, wherever you are.
